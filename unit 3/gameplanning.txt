1. Crystal Quest is an adventure game where players 
explore a magical world filled with hidden treasures 
and mysterious creatures. The goal is to collect powerful crystals while 
solving puzzles and avoiding dangers. Players can unlock new areas and 
abilities as they progress.
The game is advertised as a fun exploration, 
strategy, and adventure kind of game.

2.  The game uses simple keyboard controls.
The W, A, S, D keys move the character up, left, down, 
and right around the map. Pressing the E key lets the player 
interact with objects such as doors, treasure chests, and characters. 
The Space Bar allows the player to jump over obstacles and reach new areas.

 3. The first game object is the Magic Crystal. 
Players collect these crystals to gain points,
unlock abilities, and complete game objectives.
